# Photographs used by Direction D — attribution

**These are not Jacob's photographs, and Cairn has no photo library until Phase 6.** They are here
because Jacob ruled on 2026-09-12 that a *direction board* may render the target state: his reaction
to rendered pixels is the selection gate, and nobody can react to an empty box. `DESIGN.md` Rule B —
*a screen may only show what exists* — continues to govern what gets **built**.

Two rules were applied when fetching them, and both matter:

1. **Every picture is of the real place it is shown against.** Skradinski Buk for day 8's Krka
   excursion, Stephansdom for Vienna, the Széchenyi baths for day 13. Generic stock standing in for
   a destination would be worse than no image at all, so the matcher in `index.html` is deliberately
   narrow and a stop with no honest match simply gets no picture.
2. **Every picture is freely licensed and credited on screen**, not only in this file — the hero
   carries its credit under the photograph and the standing `#truth` disclosure appears on every
   grain that renders one. `audit.mjs` asserts both.
