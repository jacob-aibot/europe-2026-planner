What should happen to a trip file with no owner in it?

If someone exports a trip and sends it to you, the app correctly refuses it. But if that file carries no owner at all, the app adopts the whole thing as yours - and 91 of the activities inside stay quietly stamped with the other person's name, with nothing on screen saying so. Nothing leaks and nothing breaks; the app would just be telling you a trip is yours when it isn't.

  1. Refuse anything that isn't provably yours
  2. Accept it, but visibly badge the whole trip as imported from someone else
  3. Leave it until real accounts exist in Phase 3

Do you want an "accept" control?
